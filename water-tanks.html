Buying guide

Choosing the right water tank size

Consider household demand, water supply interruptions, available installation space and intended use. Larger tanks can provide a bigger reserve but require suitable access and installation space.

  • Measure the available area before ordering.
  • Choose capacity according to expected demand.
  • Confirm delivery access for large tanks.
  • Ask for the final delivered price for your location.
